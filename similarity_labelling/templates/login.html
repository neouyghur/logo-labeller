Welcome to the Logo Similarity User Study

Thank you for participating! You will see pairs of logos side by side and decide whether they look similar. Each pair takes only a few seconds, and a session is 30 pairs.

Our aim is to bridge the gap between how humans perceive visual similarity and how artificial intelligence understands it. We are building a large-scale image retrieval benchmark: a standardized test of how accurately and efficiently computer vision models search for and match images. Your judgments become part of its ground-truth dataset, used to evaluate and improve the next generation of visual search algorithms.

This research is conducted by Osman Tursun, Yusuf Efe Ozudogru, Efe Buke and Sinan Kalkan.

Your data. We store your Google email address and name, only to sign you in and to issue your certificate, together with your answers. Nothing else is collected. Before analysis your answers are linked only to a code; your email and name are never published. The data is used exclusively for academic research.
