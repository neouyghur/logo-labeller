Welcome to the Logo Similarity User Study

Thank you for participating! You will see pairs of logos side by side and decide whether they look similar. Each pair takes only a few seconds, and a session is {{ batch_pages }} pairs.

Our aim is to bridge the gap between how humans perceive visual similarity and how artificial intelligence understands it. We are building a large-scale image retrieval benchmark: a standardized test of how accurately and efficiently computer vision models search for and match images. Your judgments become part of its ground-truth dataset, used to evaluate and improve the next generation of visual search algorithms.

This research is conducted by Osman Tursun, Yusuf Efe Ozudogru, Efe Buke, Clinton Fookes and Sinan Kalkan.

Your data. Your annotations will become part of a ground-truth dataset used to evaluate and compare image retrieval methods. We do not collect any personally identifying information beyond your email address and name, used only to sign you in and to issue your certificate, and two optional questions: your age range and design experience. Your answers are stored under a code, never published with your name or email, and used exclusively for academic research. You must be 18 or older to take part.

After two optional questions, a short guide opens before your first pair. Contact details are at the bottom of the page.
