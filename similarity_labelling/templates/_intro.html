Welcome! 👋

You'll see 30 pairs of logos. For each pair, tell us whether the two logos look similar. It takes about 3 minutes, and you get a certificate at the end.

First, a 1-minute guide on what we mean by “similar”.

The one question

If you saw one of these logos briefly, could you mistake it for the other, or would it strongly remind you of the other?

{{ duo(bird("#2457c5"), bird("#159c8c"), "Similar → Yes", "yes") }}

Logos can be similar without being identical. Here only the colour changed.

What to look at

Let these shape your overall impression. No need to score each one.

Colour, rarely on its own

Colour is a trap

Both are red, but the shapes have nothing in common.

{{ duo(star("#d23a2a"), script("Kivo", "#d23a2a"), "Not similar → No", "no") }}

Colour matters, but it should rarely decide your answer alone.

Look past the photo

Blur, cropping, size or background come from how the image was collected, not from the design.

{{ duo(bird("#2457c5"), bird("#2457c5"), "Same design → Yes", "yes", rough=true) }}

Also ignore:

  • whether you know the brand
  • whether you like it
  • what the company sells

Design, not words

Different words in the same layout and font can look very similar…

{{ duo(wordmark("NOVA"), wordmark("LUMA"), "Similar → Yes", "yes") }}

…and the same word in totally different styles can look different.

{{ duo(wordmark("NOVA"), script("Nova", "#3a9a5b", leaf=true), "Not similar → No", "no") }}

Your three answers

Yes Yhighly similar: could be mistaken for each other
No Ndissimilar: little in common beyond surface traits
Unsure Uin between, or an image won't load

Go with your first instinct. If you deliberate for long, the pair is probably in the middle, so choose Unsure.
