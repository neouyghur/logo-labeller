What counts as similar?

Could you mistake one for the other if you saw it briefly, or does one strongly remind you of the other? Judge what you see, not what you know about the companies.

Look at:

  • overall shape and silhouette
  • layout of icon and text
  • symbols and imagery
  • typography
  • style (flat, outlined, 3D…)
  • color, but rarely on its own

Ignore: brand familiarity, image quality (resolution, cropping, background) and what the text means.

Yes: highly similar · Unsure: in between, or an image won't load · No: dissimilar.
